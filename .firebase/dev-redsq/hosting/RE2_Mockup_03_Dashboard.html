The Verdict
This is a strong location for Kohi, with one big caveat.
127 E 7th St sits in one of the best corridors in Manhattan for a concept like yours. The foot traffic is excellent, the demographic skews heavily toward your target (25-38, above-median income, health-conscious), and the evening economy is thriving.

The concern is competition density. There are 14 specialty coffee and tea shops within a 4-block radius. Your matcha-to-sake pivot is genuinely differentiated, but your daytime traffic will face real headwinds. The numbers work if you can convert 15% of evening customers into daytime regulars. That's aggressive but achievable with your sourcing story.
