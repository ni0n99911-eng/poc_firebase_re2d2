UX-6 · Spec Meta-line Component NEW
The meta-line appears directly under every score display (score ring, score tile, or score chip). Typography: 11px, muted grey (#8A95A8). The entire line is the tooltip trigger — hover on desktop, tap on mobile. No separate ⓘ icon needed; the meta-line itself is the affordance.

Component rules
Relative date format: "today" · "2 days ago" · "last week" · "March 28" (absolute date if >14 days old).
Tooltip trigger: hover/tap on the entire meta-line — not a separate icon. The line itself is the affordance. Tooltip appears above (never below — above score area is clipped on mobile).
Tooltip copy: Locked verbatim per BR-5 §5.5 — UX owns visual treatment only, Brain owns copy.
Placement: Directly under the score number/grade. No gap above the path label or verdict row — meta-line is the last element under the score display.
Surfaces: Dashboard score tiles (each tile gets its own meta-line under Scored date) · Location IQ page score ring · Fit IQ page score ring · Business Case Summary tab score chips.
